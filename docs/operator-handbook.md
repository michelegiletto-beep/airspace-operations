# Operator handbook

Sign in with an administrator-provisioned organisation identity. The application opens an empty operational workspace. All timestamps are UTC unless a local IANA time zone is explicitly shown. A missing feed or empty overlay does not establish clear airspace, favourable weather or absence of regulatory change.

Authorisations: create a user-entered proposed operation with jurisdiction, legal route, overall owner, separate airspace owner, ConOps and aircraft configuration. Record genuine evidence with provenance, type and content checksum; the attachment endpoint calculates its own digest. Assign authorised reviewers. Missing mandatory inputs block review. The creator or last preparer cannot approve the revision. A controlled JSON or HTML export contains record versions, evidence, missing inputs, actor and digest; it supplies no invented signature or authority stamp.

Permissions: create separate instruments for competent-authority operational authorisation, site/airspace permission, U-space authorisation, declaration or LUC privilege. Record issuer, validity, scope and receipt evidence. Internal case approval is not any of these instruments. A package is not Submitted until documentary receipt or an explicitly documented manual submission is referenced. Store original correspondence without altering its legal status.

Regulatory Library: filter registered sources, inspect licence, status and retrieval evidence, then retrieve using jurisdiction and operation date. Source quotations, anchors and machine interpretation are separate. Historical/future questions abstain where verified temporal coverage is missing. Select the methodology edition when needed. Retrieving a passage is not proof that it governs your operation. Review coverage and contradictions with an accountable specialist.

Safety Evidence: build a requirements matrix, link design/simulation/laboratory/field evidence and preserve aircraft/software configuration. The SORA workbench separates2.0 and2.5. Only explicitly implemented source-bound calculations run; incomplete methodology support never issues an overall GRC/ARC/SAIL compliance determination. Sensor and traffic-display information is not certified DAA evidence.

Airspace: record candidate sites and GeoJSON geometry with units, source precision, uncertainty and vertical reference. Spatial screening uses WGS84 geodesic calculations. Unknown altitude reference, stale zones, absent terrain conversion or missing population/obstacle data remain findings. A candidate route stays separate from an approved route.

Scheduling: supply a complete versioned input snapshot, including aircraft readiness/battery budget, personnel qualification validity/duty policy, site capacity, permission instruments, route version, weather, airspace and U-space applicability. CP-SAT proposes assignments; a separate verifier checks them. A feasible solution and a proven optimum are distinguished. Reservation requires matching persisted records, current versions and independent review, then rechecks overlaps transactionally. Reservation never releases dispatch or executes a flight. Native scheduler revision is a supplied configuration revision; `input_record_versions` binds the actual database revisions independently.

Operations and incidents: telemetry imports require authorised real records and a rights attestation. Live and Historical Replay are separate modes. Duplicate, contradictory, future and delayed events are handled explicitly. Missing monitoring policy means no arbitrary stale threshold. Alerts must be acknowledged by an accountable operational user; sent, delivered, acknowledged and action completed are distinct fields. Incident checklists come only from current approved procedures. Missing procedures or contact sources require accountable escalation.

Deployment Planning: supply dated costs and capacity with provenance (fact, customer-provided or analyst assumption). Forecast formulas stay inspectable; missing inputs stay blank. A commercial scenario cannot override an evidence or permission blocker. Unsaved browser work is retained only within the signed-in browser session and revision; save before requesting consequential state changes.
