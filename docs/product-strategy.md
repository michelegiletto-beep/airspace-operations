# Product strategy

Buyer roles are heads of regulatory approvals, accountable operations managers and safety leaders. Regulatory specialists maintain applicability and source provenance; safety/engineering teams supply configuration-specific evidence; planners maintain constraints and readiness; pilots acknowledge reviewed procedures; fleet managers maintain serviceability records; incident coordinators own response records; business development analysts enter dated deployment assumptions. The software preserves the ownership boundary between an airspace workstream and the complete authorisation case.

Implementation responsibilities: System Lab supplies the installable core, schemas, verification mechanisms and maintenance documentation. The adopting organisation supplies lawful operational records, competent reviewers, accepted procedures, data-provider contracts, security administration and regulatory/operational acceptance. Authorities and providers retain their distinct decision roles. Integration partners may include official aeronautical data providers, aircraft telemetry vendors, identity providers and designated U-space providers, subject to actual interface and rights review. No partnership is implied.

Pilot outcomes must be measured from real customer-approved records: traceable source coverage, time to find missing evidence, revision/review cycle duration, avoidable resource conflicts, restoration success and staff usability findings. No numeric improvement, approval timeline, demand or ROI is claimed before measurement.

Deployment options are self-hosted portability for evaluation, PostgreSQL-based organisation deployment after acceptance, or a managed deployment under explicit contracts and data-processing arrangements. Possible services include installation, source-pack reconciliation, integration implementation and reviewer training. The permissive downloadable core remains usable without compulsory proprietary hosting. Pricing and market comparisons are intentionally absent because no researched commercial evidence has been supplied.
