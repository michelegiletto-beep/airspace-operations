# Product legal-applicability assessment

This is an engineering issue register, not a legal opinion. Determine duties for the actual adopting legal entity, deployment, data subjects and operational service with qualified counsel and the accountable aviation specialist. Do not infer universal applicability from inclusion in this register.

GDPR: user identities, personnel qualifications, contacts and potentially telemetry can be personal data. Define controller/processor roles, lawful bases, minimisation, access, retention, deletion, subject rights, transfers, breach handling and any DPIA needs. Current private records are not intended for public model training. A deployment requiring automated erasure must close the documented implementation gap first.

Part-IS: assess the exact organisation certificate/activity, relevant information-security implementing/delegated regulations and applicability dates; a generic UAS software vendor or each operator must not automatically be described as Part-IS approved. Link any adopted requirements to current primary EASA/EUR-Lex provisions and the organisation's certificate scope.

AI Act: classify actual provider/deployer roles and intended use of the experimental evidence classifier and local retrieval. Current learned outputs do not control aircraft or grant permissions. That boundary alone does not complete an AI Act classification. Review applicable prohibited uses, transparency, literacy, high-risk classification, dates, model/data rights and product integration for the real use case.

Cybersecurity/product/occurrence requirements: assess the Cyber Resilience Act and any relevant NIS2 national transposition, aviation reporting rules, product liability/conformity obligations and customer contractual requirements. Directives require jurisdiction-specific transposition analysis. No pending proposal or unreviewed framework is enforced as adopted operational law. Current implementation has no legal compliance or certification claim.

Primary starting texts for review: EUR-Lex CELEX32016R0679 (GDPR),32024R1689 (AI Act),32022L2555 (NIS2),32024R2847 (Cyber Resilience Act),32014R0376 (occurrence reporting), and EASA information-security rule register. These references are research dependencies until their current consolidated versions and actual applicability are verified.
