# Revision review

The first candidate implemented persistent mechanisms but its public page offered little visual evidence. The interface exposed many generic record editors rather than complete operator workflows. Its trained literal-language student was experimental and inferior to a deterministic baseline; it did not demonstrate the requested LLM-led orchestration. Publication understated the relationship to the System Labs multiagentic programme and omitted the requested owned animated companions. Maintenance, shared-fleet coordination and cross-border operating segments were insufficiently developed.

The revision addresses those defects in code and publishing. The programme's public dossier identifies task contracts, scoped state, capability admission, receipts and reconciliation, and explicitly states that no general-purpose public SDK is distributed. Airspace implements compatible domain contracts from scratch; proprietary trading components and SPINS weights are not copied or relicensed. User-owned companion imagery is separately attributed and remains proprietary.

0.1.0-rc.1 stays frozen. New work is verified and released under 0.2.0-rc.1, with the original baseline plus SPECIFICATION_V2_ADDENDUM.md. Remaining legal/domain or operational acceptance gaps must stay visible. No email or human outreach is part of this work.
