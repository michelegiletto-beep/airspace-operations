# Regulatory coverage and use

Checked on 27 October 2026. This engineering release contains a researched European foundation. Luxembourg and Italy are research packs requiring qualified national review; neither is described as validated. Other European country packs and a separate UK pack are not implemented.

The source catalogue registers Regulations (EU) 2018/1139, 2019/947, 2019/945, 2021/664, 2021/665 and 2021/666. Direct EUR-Lex requests returned HTTP 202 challenge responses. The downloader rejects these responses rather than ingesting them as legal text. The original OJ texts and complete amendment reconciliation therefore remain a coverage gap.

Actual EASA UAS June 2026 and U-space May 2024 structured XML archives were downloaded and checksummed. Their text extraction retains eRules section identifiers, original legal metadata, paragraphs and table rows. Consolidated reference material is distinguished from authentic OJ text. Binding provisions, acceptable means of compliance and guidance retain distinct legal-status values. UAS material includes both the explicitly identified SORA 2.5 AMC1 and the retained SORA 2.0 AMC1bis. The parser uses the actual section and parent identifiers to preserve that boundary. A methodology filter can restrict retrieval; a result containing multiple editions explicitly requires an edition selection.

The EASA UAS publication page states that its June 2026 revision incorporates ED Decision 2025/018/R. The EASA UAS register also refers to a corrigendum. Its exact effect and completeness of the consolidation require qualified review. The U-space source includes the 2023/203 amendment and associated 22 February 2026 applicability statement. These records do not establish local U-space designation or provider availability.

The Luxembourg DAC SORA page was actually retrieved. It displays a 21 October 2024 update date and must be reconciled with SORA 2.5 and current DAC procedures. The Italian ENAC operational-authorisation page was actually retrieved in its original Italian; it displays a 14 August 2026 update date. No unreviewed translation is presented as authoritative English. National source redistribution and training rights are not cleared, so these snapshots remain local research material.

The operational database starts empty. Regulatory snapshots are source material, not operational activity. The complete source manifest and per-source last-check outcomes distinguish registered, downloaded, parsed and reviewed states. Missing checks and retrieval failures are not evidence that nothing changed.

Temporal applicability is deliberately narrow: ingested EASA consolidation passages are available for the retrieval-date snapshot only. Article-specific dates remain preserved as raw upstream metadata, and are not converted into unsupported earlier or future validity intervals. Historical and future questions abstain where no date-reviewed version is installed. A reviewer may import separately verified intervals; those versions must remain independently traceable.

Retrieval applies organisation scope, jurisdiction, legal status and relevant dates before lexical or neural ranking. EU material is excluded for the UK. An exact source quotation, upstream link, snapshot SHA-256, eRules section and local block identifier accompany results. Locally generated block identifiers resolve against the preserved snapshot, rather than pretending to be upstream website fragments. Returned provisions are evidence for review; the application does not issue legal conclusions or operating permissions.

EUROCONTROL states that EAD Basic information is for general purposes and must not be used operationally. The product does not connect to EAD Basic as an operational feed. EAD operational integration requires an agreement, permitted-purpose review, credentials and interface validation.
