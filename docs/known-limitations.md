# Known limitations and acceptance blockers

The original master specification is not fully accepted. The engineering candidate contains working services and tests; capabilities.json and REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.csv preserve the incomplete scope. No stable, certified or production-ready status is claimed.

Software/assurance gaps: Docker/PostgreSQL were unavailable on the local Windows host. Their actual isolated Ubuntu runner verification subsequently passed: application/database containers, worker heartbeat and API-created job,16 PostgreSQL extension/concurrency/dispatch/migration checks and pg_dump/pg_restore. See release-evidence/v0.2.0-rc.1/ci-verification.json. This does not establish production-scale resilience or operator acceptance. Full recurrence/charging optimisation, all SORA steps/calculations, complete route-generation/GIS datasets, automatic retention/erasure, complete route-specific submission templates, interactive offline operation, full occurrence-reporting rules and production-scale failure/performance assurance are incomplete. Local self-hosted SQLite services and restoration are exercised. Schema 3 adds a startup compatibility gate for actor-private companion data. Every older service must be stopped before migration; already-running clients are not fenced. See docs/upgrading-to-0.3.md. Current PostgreSQL/container verification is required for the changed revision; the cited 0.2 run remains historical evidence.

Regulatory/data gaps: the actual corpus has9,833 English structured EASA passages with exact archive/eRules/block anchors. It is a retrieval-date snapshot, with unreviewed consolidated currentness and no approved historical/future applicability timeline. Source retrieval is not legal reconciliation. EUR-Lex direct retrieval returned a challenge/HTTP202. Luxembourg/Italy official pages were fetched but the national packs are not deeply validated. Missing aircraft, personnel, weather, airspace, provider and operational records remain missing. Generic PDF ingestion is unavailable; separate cleared structured XML/HTML ingestion works. There is no proprietary or accepted live operational feed.

Model limitations: three semantic teacher attempts failed and remain preserved. The completed model routes literal shall-language; it does not infer legal obligations. Its machine-label agreement is inferior to a deterministic baseline. No qualified human ground truth or independent time/version holdout exists. Training ran in the historical environment recorded in its manifest. The current optional inference environment was upgraded after dependency scanning; old training versions are preserved as historical evidence and are not the recommended deployment environment. Only safetensors and pinned reviewed local model files are loaded; remote code is disabled.

Operational-acceptance requirements: the adopting organisation must establish data rights, operating procedures, competent reviewers, security/identity arrangements, provider contracts, country-specific legal basis and permissions, aircraft/configuration evidence, pilot qualifications and relevant acceptance tests. Provider-specific U-space network integration cannot be completed without actual documented interfaces/access; the release does not claim a universal authorisation API. Internal scheduling/reservations and advisory dispatch checks never activate a provider or aircraft.

Publication states are recorded separately as Prepared, Uploaded, Published and Independently Retrieved. An upload script or expected URL is not evidence of publication. External access is used only where real authenticated permission has been verified. Private runtime databases, keys, correspondence, evidence objects and model-download caches are excluded from public assets.


Revision 0.2 adds genuine local generative orchestration and constrained maintenance/shared-fleet/country/dispatch workflows, with actual execution and isolated fault tests. The local controller uses cooperative generation cancellation and bounded calls; arbitrary native-code preemption, OS process quotas and production queue SLOs require deployment-specific engineering. Cross-country readiness consumes documented instruments and source reviews, not a universal European authorisation API. The50-entry country catalogue is geographical registration, with no automatically validated national pack. The source currentness recheck on 28 October 2026 leaves the original cleared corpus unchanged.


## Existing public-site verification boundary

The scoped Airspace dossier, catalogue integration, screenshots and downloads pass their targeted checks. The complete pre-existing System Lab site suite has five inherited unrelated failures: built prior source 61 passed / 5 failed; revised source 64 passed / the same 5 failed. See release-evidence/v0.2.0-rc.1/site-baseline-comparison.json and preserved command outputs. This is not a claim that the entire site suite passes. No unrelated project content or assertions were weakened to conceal those failures.


Companion generation reservations now persist across retries and crashes, with one or two reservations per request. Uncertain interrupted work consumes its reservation; recorded reservation counts are not proven completed generations. Older pending turns without valid accounting must be sent again as new requests. Live provider access, qualified national validation and the remaining original requirements remain open.
