# Release 0.3.0-rc.1

Engineering candidate for self-hosted civilian UAS preparation and decision support. Full original requirements, stable release and operational acceptance remain incomplete.

Private local-model conversations now support persistent personal tasks and typed previews for internal draft cases and bounded workflows. An operator must explicitly confirm a proposed action. Current tenant, actor, role, scope and input versions are checked by the server. No aircraft commands, external correspondence, authority decisions or self-approval are added.

The owned companion animates in normal motion mode, including a genuine animated fallback. Pause and reduced-motion settings take precedence. The interface has a restrained reactive background, clearer guidance and removal of repeated next-step lists. Current browser checks exercised a real local-model reply, a confirmed personal release task and database/audit persistence at actual mobile and desktop widths. These are software-help records, not fictional missions or operator demonstrations.

Generation reservations are durable across retries and crashes. Uncertain interrupted work consumes its reservation; missing historical accounting fails closed. Stale workers cannot clear a newer worker's active conversation turn. Reservations and proven returned generations are recorded separately. No new student training or model-quality advantage is claimed.

Schema 3 rejects the frozen 0.2 server before it can open new private companion data. Stop every old process before migration and preserve a consistent database, objects and audit-key backup. Mixed-version rolling deployment and removal of compatibility markers are unsafe. Read docs/upgrading-to-0.3.md.

Commands, failures, model lineage, source and rights review, browser evidence, current tests and independently verified archive results accompany this candidate. Optional pinned pretrained weights are installed separately. A new operational workspace remains empty. National validation, lawful live feeds, approved operator procedures and accountable review are still required. The unchanged experimental student remains below its deterministic baseline and is not promoted.
