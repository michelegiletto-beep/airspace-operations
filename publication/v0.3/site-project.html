Work with the same operational records

Start with the operation you intend to carry out. Add actual resources and evidence, resolve missing information and send the relevant records for review.

Missions and resources

Record proposed missions, routes, operating windows, aircraft and personnel. Build scheduling proposals with a separate verifier before reserving resources.

Authorisations and evidence

Keep the ConOps, requirements, supporting documents, authority correspondence and review decisions with their versions and sources.

Fleet and maintenance

Link aircraft configurations to source-defined maintenance programmes, measured use, work orders and independent release evidence.

Country coordination

Review the countries on a route, applicable legal instruments, local conditions, handovers and explicit aircraft-sharing agreements.

Incidents and procedures

Assign ownership, follow approved operator procedures and preserve event records, notifications and corrective actions.

Deployment planning

Compare documented costs and analyst assumptions alongside regulatory requirements, resource limits and unresolved evidence.

Internal review, authority permission, U-space permission, pilot acknowledgement and actual flight execution are distinct records. The application does not command aircraft or activate provider services.

Local assistance and saved tasks

Ask Indigo for help with the workspace you are using. Conversations and personal tasks are saved privately for the signed-in user. The optional local language model selects permitted administrative guidance and recorded facts; it does not generate flight instructions or legal permissions.

Review the proposed action before confirming a personal task, draft case or internal workflow. Cases and workflows become organisation records. The interface shows queued work, actual worker progress, missing inputs, expired proposals and model or budget failures.

The owned companion animates during normal use. The existing renderer supports WebGL and animated 2D output, with a procedural animated fallback. Pause and reduced-motion preferences take precedence. The reactive background responds to pointer and keyboard focus while leaving controls readable.

Connection to the System Lab programme

The application uses a new shared systemlab_agentic coordination core and existing owned companion presentation technology. Its aviation tools, data contracts and review rules are specific to Airspace Operations. The original five renderer and artwork resources retain reserved rights; the new adapter and application code use Apache-2.0.

Coverage and remaining limits

Regulatory sources

The library preserves 9,833 actual EASA passages with editions, checksums and anchors. This dated corpus does not establish complete or permanently current European law. Luxembourg and Italy require qualified reconciliation, other country entries do not imply validated coverage, and UK applicability is separate.

Experimental student

The unchanged literal-language student achieved 81.82% agreement on 77 unreviewed machine labels, below the deterministic literal-word baseline's 100%. These are not legal correctness scores. The model remains experimental and unpromoted; no new training or benchmark improvement is claimed.

Operational acceptance

Real operator records, lawful feeds, approved procedures, competent review, national applicability and permissions are still required. Installation implies no accepted live telemetry, weather, airspace or contracted U-space connection. The platform is not certified separation, Detect and Avoid or air traffic control.
