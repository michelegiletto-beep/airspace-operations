# Data register

Production: initially empty tenant records, evidence references, source ingestion jobs and telemetry. Operators supply lawful records. No artificial operational dataset is seeded. Local runtime storage is excluded from source releases.

Regulatory: source manifests and content hashes in sources/. Original public documents may be downloaded locally subject to issuer terms; source rights and applicability are recorded independently. Original-language content is not silently translated. No claim of complete EU or national coverage.

Training: only source-derived passages with provenance and explicit machine annotation status. Dataset and model cards describe actual runs and licences. Test fixtures never enter the training or production stores. Evaluation is not expert approval.

Personal data: user emails and personnel records are tenant-confidential. Administrators control lawful purposes and retention. Passwords and session tokens are never retained in plaintext. Public release tooling excludes local databases, source caches, uploaded evidence, logs and credentials.


Revision0.2 software evaluation: a separate private runtime organisation with an explicitly local review identity executed a public-source question through the actual Qwen controller, retrieval, reranker and experimental student. Its recorded receipts, quotations and timings are software evidence, not flights, customers, authority decisions or human-validated labels. Public screenshots contain an empty operational workspace and this genuine source-research workflow. Qwen pretrained weights were explicitly downloaded locally and verified; they are excluded from the application archive. Existing student weights and training lineage are unchanged. Owned companion presentation resources retain reserved rights and exact hashes.
