# Source register

Research and retrieval date: **27 October 2026**. This human-readable summary accompanies the [machine catalogue](sources/catalogue.json), [coverage matrix](sources/coverage.json), [rights review](sources/rights-review.json) and structured corpus in `sources/corpus.json` (expanded by `scripts/prepare_corpus.py` from the repository's compressed corpus). A registered source is not necessarily downloaded, current, rights-cleared or domain-validated.

## Provenance and citations

The UAS archive SHA-256 is `0d35166d374084346d6f4b3ac962102ef75d13963ab8d1d0360cb988ab85dc54`; the U-space archive SHA-256 is `0db50106a4d58ab95f21228eef781ef63f5098b9c4550e7c15b2e9cfc42286a5`. Original research snapshots are retained locally under `sources/snapshots/` and excluded from public packages; the official archive links above permit fresh retrieval. The public corpus retains cleared extracted text and original checksums. [The extraction-change record](sources/corpus-change-log.json) distinguishes parser/provenance corrections from changes to source text.

A citation combines source ID, edition, original checksum, source URL and the original eRules section plus local extraction-block identifier. Local block identifiers resolve against the preserved snapshot; they are not invented upstream website fragments. Paragraph and table-row locations remain distinct, including short or repeated table values. Binding legislation, AMC, guidance and national procedures must not be treated as equivalent legal instruments. Retrieved quotations remain source evidence; machine interpretation and reviewer-approved interpretation are separate records.

The [execution ledger](release-evidence/regulatory-neural-execution.json) records successful and failed commands. [Isolated-parser evidence](release-evidence/isolated-source-parser.json) records actual archive parsing. [Registered-monitor evidence](release-evidence/registered-monitor-real-check.json) records fresh, unmocked HTTPS checks of the UAS publication page and U-space XML archive; both succeeded and left the public corpus unchanged. Those checks establish retrieval and parsing, not operational acceptance. Private worker outputs stay in tenant-scoped runtime stores with check history and preserved last-success timestamps.

## Rights, language and coverage

EASA's [copyright notice](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/copyright-disclaimer) was retrieved and permits acknowledged reproduction except where otherwise stated. The recorded release scope is acknowledged EASA/EU text with document-specific restrictions respected; logos and illustrations are excluded. National research snapshots are not cleared for public redistribution or training. Apache-2.0 project code does not relicense source documents, paid standards or model weights. See the [rights review](sources/rights-review.json) for these separate decisions.

The two XML corpora and Luxembourg snapshot are original English material. ENAC snapshots remain original Italian. No English machine translation is represented as authoritative source text. Newly authored interface text and documentation are English.

The European foundation and both Luxembourg and Italy packs **Require Domain Review**. Neither national pack is validated. Missing items include complete amendment/date reconciliation, current national zones and procedures, operational aeronautical-data rights, qualified review and product-specific privacy, Part-IS and AI Act applicability assessment. Other national packs are not validated; the UK requires a separate legal pack and EU retrieval does not apply to it.

Date filtering currently exposes the XML consolidations for their checked retrieval-date snapshot only. Raw article applicability metadata is preserved without inferring unverified historical or future intervals. Unsupported dates or jurisdictions must produce missing-evidence states or abstention. Successful retrieval and notification silence do not establish continuing currentness. See [regulatory coverage](docs/regulatory-coverage.md), [ingestion administration](docs/regulatory-ingestion.md) and [legal applicability](docs/legal-applicability.md).
