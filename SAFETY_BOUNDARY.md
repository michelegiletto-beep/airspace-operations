# Safety boundary

This software manages records and supports decisions. It does not control aircraft, provide certified separation, supply a certified Detect and Avoid function, act as an air traffic control or U-space service provider, or issue authority permissions. Software tests confer none of those authorities.

Regulatory applicability, safety review, competent-authority operating basis, local permissions, U-space authorisation, internal dispatch, pilot acknowledgement and actual execution are separate instruments. Recorded authority decisions require issuer and documentary evidence. A reviewer cannot approve their own record. Missing evidence, unknown vertical reference, stale inputs or unknown permission scope remain blockers.

SORA methodology editions are never mixed. Unimplemented decision tables remain incomplete and require qualified review. Cooperative traffic does not describe all surrounding traffic. Displayed routes are proposals. The neural subsystem is experimental and cannot override deterministic gates. Approved operator and aircraft-specific procedures are the only source of emergency instructions.

Operational deployment requires appropriate data rights, operator procedures, qualified regulatory and safety review, customer acceptance, security configuration and applicable permissions. These requirements are separate from software defects and release build success.
