# Companion and experience revision

The original SPECIFICATION.md and the previous revision baseline remain unchanged. This addendum records the user's further instructions on 28 October 2026 for version 0.3.0-rc.1. Existing published candidates retain their original bytes and tags.

The user requests removal of slogans and recognisable formulaic AI presentation, including decorative hashtags, slashes, dashes and promotional phrasing. The application must guide an inexperienced operator through real tasks using a local LLM, persistent task keeping, workflow preparation and visible action proposals. The companion must animate during normal operation instead of becoming a fixed image. The experience should include a restrained reactive background and useful motion aligned with the System Lab multiagentic programme. Publication must be maintained professionally. A recurring cycle must review, improve, verify and, when justified by passing gates, publish distinct new candidates. Email and contact with people remain prohibited.

Removing decorative punctuation does not mean modifying a URL, code identifier, source quotation, legal reference, unit or machine-readable format. Standard document structure is retained in technical files. No claim is made that an automated phrase list can prove the absence of every stylistic defect. Browser review and the recurring cycle supplement bounded checks.

This revision does not waive incomplete original requirements, safety review, national validation, provider access or operational acceptance. The product remains an engineering candidate while those requirements remain open.
